Tildy

Privacy

Draft of November 14, 2026. Not yet reviewed by a lawyer.

The short version

Tildy reads your account balances so it can tell you how much you can spend today. It does not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and sites.

What Tildy collects

  • An account identifier. When you sign in with Apple, Tildy receives an identifier for your account. It does not ask Apple for your name or email address.
  • Bank account details, through Plaid. The bank's name, each account's name and type, and its current balance. Tildy does not store your transactions and never sees your bank username or password.
  • A connection token. Plaid gives Tildy a token that lets it ask for your balances again. It is stored encrypted on Tildy's server and never sent to your phone.

What stays on your iPhone

Your pay schedule, your bills, the money you set aside, and anything you tell Tildy about your spending are stored on your device, not on Tildy's server.

Who else handles your data

  • Plaid connects Tildy to your bank. Plaid's handling of your information is described in its End User Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase hosts Tildy's server and database.
  • Apple provides sign-in, notifications and subscriptions.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect a bank at any time in Settings. Deleting your account in Settings removes your bank connections and your account from Tildy's server and erases the data on your device.
